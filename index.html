Good to know

Before you download.

Free to download, with optional Nostalgia Camera Pro for more eras and color options.

Is this a real CCD sensor?

No. These are software looks inspired by older camera aesthetics. The app does not change your phone’s physical camera sensor.

Do I need an account to shoot?

No. You can shoot without an account, and photo processing happens on your device.

Are all eras included for free?

No. Optional Pro purchases unlock more eras and color options. Check the app for the current selection and prices.
