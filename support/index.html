Where are my photos stored?

On your device only. The app does not require an account and does not upload your camera roll to our servers.

How do I restore purchases?

Open Settings → Restore purchases (or the same action on the paywall).

How do I cancel a subscription?

Manage or cancel in iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple.
